// research

I research how minds, brains, and machines represent visual and social concepts.

Mental & neural motifs

Some of my work aims to characterize shared and individual-specific patterns in how people mentally organize semantic content. My current focus is applying behaviorally-derived, idiosyncratic semantic similarity spaces to neural decoding.

Social knowledge organization

How is social knowledge structured in mind and brain? I investigate how semantic distance organizes mental representations of social roles and identities, drawing on behavioral data, semantic dementia case studies, and cross-modal conceptual spaces.

The nature of representations

How do our represenations of semantic content accommodate and access the many kinds of information associated with a concept? What are the mathematical principles behind how this is accomplished in the brain (and its models)?
